TipTape: Server Tip Tracker

Cash, Credit, Income & Tax

Close out a shift the way you count it at the station: cash fold, card tips, tip-out, hours. Tip Tape prints each number on a slip and tells you tonight's take-home and your real hourly rate.

Your week lives on one register tape: week to date, every shift you worked, and the tips, tip-out and wages that add up to it. When last month's tips are due to payroll, the amount and the due date are printed right there.

FREE
• Close out a shift in four numbers: cash, card, tip-out and hours, or start and end times with an unpaid break
• Tip-out as a dollar amount, a percent of sales or a percent of tips, plus tip-in from the pool
• Take-home and effective hourly rate for every shift, week and month
• History by workweek, and monthly reports with tips as a percent of sales
• The monthly tip report: what to report to your employer, the $20 rule and the due date
• iCloud backup to your own account, and a reminder to log your shift

PRO
• Every job and position, each with its own wage and tip-out rule, with totals per job and combined
• A monthly tip statement PDF for payroll with the fields the IRS asks for, a year summary for your tax preparer, and CSV of every shift
• Pay checks: did your cash wage plus tips reach the minimum wage each week, and your no-tax-on-tips deduction estimate

Made for servers, bartenders, bussers and barbacks. No account to lose, no ads. Records and estimates only, not tax or legal advice.
