Staywhile Cat Focus Timer

Body doubling with sleepy cats

Some work only gets done when someone is in the room with you. Staywhile puts the someone there.

Turn the dial to how long you'll stay. Outlines appear on the windowsill, the rug and the armchair, each tagged with the minute that cat will walk in: one guest for a short sitting, five for an hour. Start, and get on with your work. At those minutes the cats arrive, look around, and curl up asleep while you keep going.

STAY, AND THEY STAY
Finish the sitting and the room is drawn into your sketchbook: who came, where they slept, the minute each one arrived. Stop early and they wake up and leave. Nothing is marked as a failure and nothing dies; the room is just quieter.

LONGER SITTINGS, RARER GUESTS
Biscuit turns up for almost anyone. Juniper only watches the window if you stay 25 minutes. The Professor needs 45 and the lamp on. Fifteen cats in all, each with its own face, coat and habits, and a guest register that keeps track of how many sittings you've shared.

MADE FOR THE DESK
• Sittings from 5 to 60 minutes, set by dragging the dial or tapping minus and plus
• A notification when time's up, so the phone can lie face down
• An optional 5, 10 or 15-minute break while the cats sleep on
• The window follows the real hour, from early light to a lamp-lit night
• Share any page of the sketchbook as a picture
• Works with VoiceOver and the largest text sizes, and on iPhone Duo open or closed
