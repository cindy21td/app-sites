Purrsist: Study Timer & Cats

Pomodoro Focus, Cozy Rewards

Purrsist is a focus timer with company. Start a session and hand-drawn cats wander into your little study, one by one, to nap beside you while you work. Finish, and they're yours to keep in the cat book. Stop early, and they wander off.

Longer sessions bring rarer cats. Earn treats for every focused minute and decorate the room: each cat has a favourite spot and a favourite thing, so what you place decides who visits. The window follows the real time of day.

Pomodoro sessions with optional breaks, a notification when time's up, gentle streaks and weekly stats. No account, no ads, no tracking. Purrsist Plus, a one-time purchase, adds two more rooms, six more cats and two-hour sessions.
