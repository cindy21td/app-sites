Twice Round Body Tracker

Tape measurements for recomp

Measure your waist twice, without looking at the first number, and Twice Round learns how much your own tape wobbles. From then on it tells you whether this week's reading is a real change or just your tape: "Within your tape", or "Down 1.1 cm since 7 Nov, beyond your band of ±1.0 cm".

A home tape reading moves by half a centimetre on its own: the tape sits a little higher, pulls a little tighter, you breathe in. Twice Round is for people who measure every week with a soft tape, through a cut, a bulk or a recomp, and want to stop reacting to that half centimetre.

YOUR OWN TAPE BAND
For the first five sessions each spot is measured twice. From those pairs Twice Round works out your technical error of measurement for that spot, the same repeatability figure anthropometrists use, and draws it on the spot's tape as a hatched band. A new reading inside the band is your tape; outside it, the change is dated. Up and down are written the same way, in the same colour. After the first five, the route asks for a second reading once a month to keep the band current.

THE ROUTE
One spot at a time, in the same order every week: a drawing of where the tape goes, your reading on a tape you slide under a hairline (or type it, or nudge it in 0.1 steps), and the second reading started blank so the first can't steer it. Centimetres or inches.

THE TAPE BOARD
Each spot is a length of tape with your weekly readings pencilled on it and your band shaded. Tap one for its weeks, the pairs it learned from and how the band is worked out.

APPLE HEALTH
Your waist (and hips, where Apple Health has a place for them) is saved to Apple Health after each route, so it outlasts the app. Weight, height and body fat are read from Health and shown beside the tape; Twice Round never works anything out from them.

FREE, FOR GOOD
Waist and hips with their bands, the full history, Apple Health, a weekly reminder and a backup file you can save anywhere. No account.

THE REST OF THE TAPE, BOUGHT ONCE
• Every other spot: chest, neck, shoulders, upper arms, forearms, thighs, calves and three of your own, each with its own band
• Same-pose photos: front, side, back, your last photo faint over the camera, a timer, kept only on this iPhone
• Your measuring sheet as a PDF or CSV for a coach or a doctor

No BMI, no body-fat estimates, no goals, no streaks. Twice Round is not a medical device: it does not diagnose, treat or advise on any condition, and the band says how repeatable your tape is, nothing about your health. Talk to a doctor before medical decisions.

The rest of the tape is a one-time purchase of $9.99 inside the app. There is no subscription and nothing renews; you pay once on your Apple Account and keep it on every device signed in to it. Restore it from the rest-of-the-tape page or from Tape and settings.
