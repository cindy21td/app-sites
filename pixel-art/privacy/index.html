Privacy policy

Gridbook Pixel Art Editor

Gridbook Pixel Art Editor is built to work on your device. It has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Data you enter

Everything you create in the app is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, the app also backs it up to your own iCloud account (a private app folder, encrypted by Apple) so you can restore it on a new iPhone. We cannot see it. To remove the backup, delete it under Settings › Apple ID › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising services. It goes online only through Apple's own services: the App Store, to check whether you have bought the premium features, and iCloud for the backup. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple sends us anonymous crash reports; you can turn that off under Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Purchases

Premium features are a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. Payments are handled by Apple and we never see your payment details. Restore the purchase on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
