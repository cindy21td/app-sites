Gridbook: Pixel Art Maker

Sprite Editor, GIF Animator

Every stroke is saved the moment you lift your finger. Drag the strip under the canvas back to any stroke and restore it, days later, after a relaunch, even after a crash.

Gridbook is a pixel art editor and sprite creator for game devs and pixel artists. It opens straight on your last sprite, with the true-size preview and the count of strokes saved. No gallery first, no pop-ups, no ads and no account.

Restoring never deletes anything. It adds a new step, so the strokes after it stay in the strip.

FREE, WITH NO LIMIT ON HOW MUCH YOU MAKE OR EXPORT
• Pixel drawing tools: pencil, eraser, fill, line, rectangle, ellipse, eyedropper, select and wand
• Sprites from 16 × 16 to 512 × 512, with up to 3 layers
• A fixed tool rail at your thumb, plus a precision cursor with an offset pointer
• Editable palettes and a full color picker with hex entry
• Stroke replay: watch how a sprite got made and share it as a video
• PNG export at any whole-number scale, so every pixel stays square
• Unlimited sprites, Recently Deleted for 30 days, and iCloud backup

PRO
• Animation: frames with per-frame timing, onion skin, GIF export and sprite sheets
• .aseprite files: open and save them, with layers and tags
• Studio tools: tilemaps, indexed color, dithering, up to 32 layers and mirror drawing

Try Pro free for 14 days. The trial starts only when you tap it, never charges and never renews. When it ends, every sprite still opens and PNG export stays free.

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. Pay once and keep it. It restores on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.
