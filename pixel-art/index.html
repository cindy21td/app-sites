Gridbook Pixel Art Editor

Restore Any Stroke You Drew

Draw pixel art one stroke at a time, and keep every stroke. Under the canvas runs a strip from your first stroke to your newest. Drag it to any point and the sprite shows exactly how it looked then.

From any stroke in the strip you can do three things. Restore the sprite to it, as a new step, so the strokes after it stay in the strip. Branch from it: Gridbook starts a second sprite with the history up to that stroke and leaves the first one as it is. Or edit that one stroke: remove it, or recolour it, and everything you drew afterwards stays exactly as you drew it. If a later fill touched the same area, Gridbook tells you before you commit. A stroke drawn before layers or frames were added or removed can be restored or branched from, but not edited.

Hold a pixel to see who painted it: the stroke number, the tool, the colour and when. Tap Show in strip to jump there. On a Mac, right-click the pixel.

Each finished stroke is written to the sprite's file the moment you lift your finger. If the app is closed or quits unexpectedly, the sprite reopens with every stroke you had finished.

The drawing tools are the ones pixel artists expect: pencil, eraser, fill, line, rectangle, ellipse, eyedropper, rectangle select and magic wand, with a fixed tool rail at your thumb and a precision cursor with an offset pointer. Sprites run from 16 × 16 to 512 × 512. Replay shows how a sprite was made, stroke by stroke, and shares it as a video.

What is free: drawing, the strip, restoring, branching, editing old strokes, who painted this, up to 3 layers, PNG export at any whole-number scale, video replay, Recently Deleted for 30 days and iCloud backup. There are no ads and no account.

Gridbook Pro is a one-time purchase, with no subscription. It adds animation (frames with their own timing, onion skin and playback), GIF export, sprite sheets and tilesets with the data files game tools read, step sheets that print a drawing as numbered steps with the colours each one adds, opening and saving .aseprite files with layers and tags, and the studio tools: tilemaps, indexed colour, dithering, up to 32 layers and mirror drawing.

Pro is free for 14 days from the day you install Gridbook. There is nothing to tap, nothing is charged and nothing renews. When the 14 days end, every sprite still opens, nothing you made is locked, and PNG export stays free. To get Pro back on a new device, tap Restore purchases in Settings or on the Pro sheet.

Gridbook runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac, with menus and keyboard shortcuts for every tool on the Mac.

Aseprite is a separate desktop editor from another developer. Gridbook reads and writes its .aseprite file format and is not affiliated with it or endorsed by it.
