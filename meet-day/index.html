Attempts Out for Powerlifting

Warm-ups called by the flight

You're eight out. Your last warm-up goes at four. Attempts Out times your warm-ups by the number of attempts left before you're called, counted from the platform, not from a clock that's wrong as soon as the flight runs late.

Copy your flight off the board: lifters in your group, groups before yours, your spot in round 1, your opener. Tap Attempt done each time anyone finishes on the platform. The count drops, the pace is measured from your taps, and every warm-up is marked on the flight track at the count you want to take it. Drag a mark to move it.

When your own lights come on, the one-minute card opens. It offers only the calls the IPF Technical Rulebook 2026 allows after a good lift or a miss, says what goes on the bar if no card goes in, and shows the loader for the weight you pick.

ON MEET DAY
- The count of attempts out, large enough to read across the warm-up room
- Next warm-up and about how long until it, from the measured pace
- The opener-change window on the track, closing at its count
- The one-minute card clock with legal calls only, and the third-deadlift changes you have left
- Plates per side drawn from the sleeve, heaviest in, exact to 0.25 kg, record discs when you need them
- Rack and safety heights, shown when that lift is up
- A meet is one file you can AirDrop to your handler

PRO, ONE PURCHASE
- The count on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, with an Attempt done button, so the phone stays in your pocket between warm-ups
- Handle a whole team from one meet file
- Every meet kept; the next one starts from your rack heights and warm-up plan

It doesn't score the meet or pick your attempts: your coach does that, and the officials decide. No account, nothing leaves your phone unless you send the file.

Attempts Out is free to use for one lifter's meet. Pro is a one-time purchase, charged to your Apple Account when you confirm it; there is no subscription and nothing renews. Restore it on a new phone from Meet room notes.
