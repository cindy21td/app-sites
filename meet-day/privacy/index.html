Privacy policy

Attempts Out for Powerlifting

Attempts Out for Powerlifting is built to work on your device. It has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Data you enter

Each meet is saved as a file on your device, in the app's folder in the Files app. A meet leaves your phone only when you send it yourself, for example by AirDrop to your handler. We cannot see any of it. The Lock Screen count (Pro) is drawn by iOS on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising services. It goes online only through Apple's own services: the App Store, to check whether you have bought the premium features. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple sends us anonymous crash reports; you can turn that off under Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Purchases

Premium features are a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. Payments are handled by Apple and we never see your payment details. Restore the purchase on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
