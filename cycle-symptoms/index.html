Tellday PMS & PMDD Tracker

Period Predictor by Symptom

Tellday tells you roughly how many days you are from your period, read from the evenings your symptoms usually arrive.

With PMDD or hard PMS, a calendar count alone can be a week out. Tellday lines up your past cycles on the day each period began and reads where tonight sits: "About 6 days before your period. Could be 5 to 8." Every estimate shows its range and how often it was right before.

THE STRIP
Each past cycle is one row of days, all aligned on the period line, with today marked on the top row. Read down today's column to see what usually happens to you at this point.

EACH EVENING
• One tap per symptom, from none to severe: irritability, anxiety, low mood or depression, cramps, migraine, bloating, or symptoms you name yourself.
• When a symptom reaches its usual level, the estimate moves that same evening.
• Pick one symptom to trace across your cycles: the days before your period it tends to arrive, and the day it usually lifts.

FIRST MINUTE
Give the date your last period started (or read period days from Apple Health), then draw your usual hard days on the strip from memory. Your own logs replace the sketch as they come in.

PRIVATE
No account and nothing to sign in to. Your chart stays on this iPhone; save a backup file wherever you choose. No ads and no analytics.

WHAT'S FREE
Check-ins, the count and its range, period days and the backup file. The Full chart adds every symptom across every cycle, the count on your Home Screen and Lock Screen, and a summary report to save or print before an appointment.

Tellday does not diagnose anything, and it is not a period calendar for avoiding or planning pregnancy. Its dates are estimates.

The Full chart is an auto-renewing subscription: $4.99 a month or $24.99 a year, with the first month free for new subscribers. Payment is taken from your Apple Account when you confirm, and the plan renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel it under Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions, and restore it from the Full chart screen with Restore.
