Privacy policy

Tellday PMS & PMDD Tracker

Tellday PMS & PMDD Tracker works entirely on your device. It has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Data on your device

Your symptom ratings, period days and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's own file. We cannot see them. If you allow it, the app reads period days from Apple Health on your device to fill in your past cycles; it never writes to Apple Health, and nothing it reads leaves your device or is used for advertising. You can save a backup file to a place you choose, such as the Files app; that copy is yours and is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes the data on the device.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising services, and it only goes online to talk to the App Store about your purchase.

Purchases

The paid features are an auto-renewing subscription bought through Apple. Payments are handled by Apple and we never see your payment details. The app asks the App Store on your device whether the subscription is active; no purchase data is sent to us. Manage or cancel it in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions, and restore it on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
