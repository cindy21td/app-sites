Privacy policy

Rentang KPR, Sewa & Investasi

Rentang KPR, Sewa & Investasi works entirely on your device. It has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Data on your device

Your plans, the figures you change and your settings are stored only on your device, as small files that are part of your device backup. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising services, and it only goes online to talk to the App Store about your purchase.

One exception: when you tap "Cek data terbaru", the app downloads a public data file (rates.json) from curiouscrane.dev. Nothing about you is sent with it; like any website, the hosting service (GitHub Pages) sees the IP address of that request.

Purchases

The paid upgrade is a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. Payments are handled by Apple and we never see your payment details. Restore the purchase on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
