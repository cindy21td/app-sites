Dates & Duty: Shift Calendar

Firefighter trades, years out

Put in the dates your family can't move. Dates & Duty shows, years ahead, which ones fall on your 24-hour shift and which platoon to trade with, so the trade gets asked for in March, not the week of the birthday.

THE YEARS BOARD
Each family date is a row and each coming year a column. Every cell is a duty tag: on duty with the off platoons on its trim band ("trade with A or B"), home, or off at your shift change the morning after. The number at the top counts the dates that land on your shift in the next 12 months.

YOUR ROTATION, NOT A CALENDAR TO TYPE
Pick 24/48, 48/96, 24/72 or the 9-day Kelly pattern, or type your own on/off cycle, then tap your platoon's row on this week's board. Shifts come from the rotation; you never enter them. Add a Kelly day every Nth shift, mark overtime, vacation or sick on the duty board, and see the month or the whole year.

THE TRADE BOOK
Log who worked for whom and the book keeps the balance: "Ortiz owes you 12 h", "you owe Reyes a shift". A traded day turns over on the board and clears the date. Mark a trade paid back with a swipe.

ASK EARLY
Set a reminder to ask for a trade 2, 4 or 6 weeks or 3 months before each date that falls on shift. The widget shows your next shift and the next family date on duty.

PRO: THE FOLD-OUT
• Years 3 to 5 for every date (free shows this year and next)
• Share the board with your house through iCloud: your partner sees the duty days and the dates and can add their own, and nobody signs up for anything
• The FLSA 7(k) work-period meter: hours in your 7 to 28-day period against the fire protection limit in 29 CFR 553.230, with trades counted the way 553.31 says. It shows the federal minimum; your contract may pay overtime sooner.

YOUR RECORDS STAY YOURS
No sign-up and no server of ours: the board lives on your phone and syncs through your own iCloud, and it works with no signal. Export the board and the trade book as a PDF any time, free.

Dates & Duty records trades for you; your battalion still approves them.
