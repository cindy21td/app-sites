Privacy policy

Cheese Guide: Mouse Puzzle

Cheese Guide: Mouse Puzzle works entirely on your device. It has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Data on your device

Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising services, and it only goes online to talk to the App Store about your purchase.

Purchases

The full game is a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. Payments are handled by Apple and we never see your payment details. Restore the purchase on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
