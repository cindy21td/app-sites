Cupback Cafe Drink, Made Home

Recipes priced by your own bag

The coffee you just bought has a label: the size, the shots, the milk, the pumps of syrup, iced or hot. Write it down with what you paid and where, and the same drink comes back reprinted for the brewer already in your cupboard (a moka pot, a cone dripper, a French press, a press brewer, a phin, a tubruk glass or an espresso machine) with the dose, the water and the steps.

Every line of the reprint carries its own price, worked out from what you actually bought: the bag of beans by its grams, the milk by its carton, the syrup by its bottle. 17 g from a $16.99 bag is 85 cents of coffee. When a bag runs out and the next one costs more, the new cups take the new price and the old ones keep theirs.

Tap Made my usual when you make one at home. Cupback counts what you didn't spend at the counter and steps a line up by it, cup by cup, toward the price of your moka pot (or dripper, or machine). The cup that crosses the pot's line is the one that paid for it.

THE LABEL
- Size by dragging across five cups, 8 to 24 oz; shots and pumps on steppers
- Milk, syrup, iced or hot, what you paid
- Where, searched in Apple Maps, and a photo if you want one
- Labels you bought before, one tap to key the same drink again
- Open any drink or cup later to fix or delete it

THE REPRINT
- The same drink for your brewer: dose, water, milk, syrup, ice
- Change the dose, water, milk and pumps for your brewer; every line re-prices
- Short steps; a step with a time starts its own timer, still running when you come back
- Each line priced from your own bag, carton and bottle

THE PAYBACK
- What your brewer has kept so far, over a line that steps up with every home cup
- Drag along the line to read any cup: its day and the running total
- This week's cups made and drinks bought, one tap from the Counter
- The two prices as two lengths on one scale
- Every drink bought and made, exported as CSV whenever you like

CUPBACK PRO
- A reprint and payback line for every brewer you own, cheapest first
- Espresso machine: the last good grind and ratio for each bag
- Made my usual from Siri, Shortcuts, the Action button or a Lock Screen button
- A Home Screen widget with this week's money kept and the payback line

The amounts are a starting point for your brewer, not a cafe's recipe, and Cupback makes no promise that a home cup tastes the same. It gives no health or caffeine advice. No account; your drinks stay on your iPhone.

Cupback Pro costs $1.99 a month or $12.99 a year, and either plan starts with a free week. Your Apple Account is charged when the free week ends, unless you cancel before then. The plan renews automatically until you turn it off at least a day before the next period, in Settings > your name > Subscriptions. A week you haven't used ends when you subscribe.
