Forest Settlement: Focus Timer

Focus game: grow a village

Focus, and a village grows in the woods.

Start a focus session and put your phone down. Before you start, the Clearing shows what the session will pay and what that buys: 25 minutes might bring 30 wood, 12 stone and 8 food, enough for a Lumber Mill. When the timer finishes, your villagers bring home wood, stone, food and gold, and you spend them on houses, farms, mills and markets. Keep focusing day after day and a campsite becomes a hamlet, then a town, then a settlement crowned by the ancient Heartwood.

Your first session starts right away, with no tutorial. Everything lives in four tabs: the Clearing, where you focus; the Village, where you build; the Chronicle of quests; and the Ledger of your focus time.

• Pomodoro-style focus timer: 10, 25, 45 or 60 minutes, or drag the woodpile to set your own length (one log is 5 minutes), with optional short breaks
• See what a session pays before you start, and what it is enough to build
• Follow a running session on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with the time left and what it pays. Keep your village and streak on the Home Screen with a widget, and start a session from Shortcuts or Siri
• 17 buildings to place and upgrade, with bonuses for good neighbours
• Villagers who need feeding, move in when there are free beds and make the village more productive
• Four seasons that change what your village harvests, and a hungry winter to plan for
• Village events with real choices, a 16-chapter Chronicle of quests and a trading post
• Streaks, a daily focus goal, weekly charts and achievements
• A living scene that follows your real clock: dawn, dusk, starry nights and daily weather
• Your village syncs between your iPhones through your own iCloud account

No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Focus is the only way to grow your village.

Optional decor packs (Harvest Fair, Lantern Night and Winter Hearth) are one-time purchases that only change how your village looks.
