Forest Settlement: Focus Timer

Pomodoro study, grow a village

Focus, and a village grows in the woods.

Start a focus session and put your phone down. When the timer finishes, your villagers bring home wood, stone and gold, and you spend them on houses, farms, mills and markets. Keep focusing day after day and a campsite becomes a hamlet, then a town, then a settlement crowned by the ancient Heartwood.

• Pomodoro-style focus timer: 10, 25, 45 or 60 minutes, or your own length, with optional short breaks
• 17 buildings to place and upgrade, with bonuses for good neighbours
• Villagers who need feeding, move in when there are free beds and make the village more productive
• Four seasons that change what your village harvests, and a hungry winter to plan for
• Village events with real choices, a 16-chapter Chronicle of quests and a trading post
• Streaks, a daily focus goal, weekly charts and achievements
• A living scene that follows your real clock: dawn, dusk, starry nights and daily weather
• Your village syncs between your iPhones through your own iCloud account

No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Focus is the only way to grow your village.

Optional decor packs (Harvest Fair, Lantern Night and Winter Hearth) are one-time purchases that only change how your village looks.
