Privacy policy

Forest Settlement: Focus Timer

Forest Settlement: Focus Timer works on your device and your own iCloud. It has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Data on your device

Your progress and settings are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, the app also keeps a copy in your own iCloud account so your other iPhones stay in sync. We cannot see either copy. Deleting the app removes the copy on the device; the iCloud copy stays with your Apple ID so reinstalling restores your progress.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising services, and it only goes online through Apple's own services: iCloud to sync your progress and the App Store for purchases.

Purchases

Optional packs are one-time in-app purchases, not subscriptions, and only change how the app looks. Payments are handled by Apple and we never see your payment details. Restore them on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
