Privacy policy

Kucing Nusantara

Kucing Nusantara works entirely on your device. It has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Data on your device

Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising services, and it only goes online to talk to the App Store about your purchase.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases: packs of in-game currency (used up as you spend them) and one-time cosmetic packs, not subscriptions. Everything they buy can also be earned by playing, except the cosmetic items. Payments are handled by Apple and we never see your payment details. Restore cosmetic packs on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
