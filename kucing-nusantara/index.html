App

Kucing Nusantara

Cozy Indonesian cat collector

Your yard is quiet. Fill the rooster bowl with food, set out things cats like, and close the app. When you come back, kampung cats are curled up in a noodle box, napping on a batik cushion, or peeking out of a cracker tin.

Cats that head home leave dried anchovies (teri) as thanks. Spend them on new things for the yard: a becak, a bakso cart, a gamelan, a hammock made from a sarong, and more.

• Collect 38 cats, including rare ones that only visit at night or in the rain
• 27 Indonesian goodies for the yard and the porch
• Remodel as a Javanese pendopo, a Balinese shrine or a Minang Rumah Gadang
• Day and night follow your clock, and the rainy season runs October to March
• Photo album and keepsakes from your regulars

No ads, no in-app purchases, no internet needed. The game is in Bahasa Indonesia.
