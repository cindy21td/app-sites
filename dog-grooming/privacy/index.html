Privacy policy

Groomer’s Dog Grooming Book

Groomer’s Dog Grooming Book is built to work on your device. It has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Data you enter

Your client book (owners, dogs, groom maps, certificate dates and notes) and any before and after photos are stored on your device, in the app's own files. With Pro and iCloud on, the app also backs them up each night to your own iCloud account so you can restore them on a new iPhone. We cannot see either copy. A report card leaves the app only when you send it yourself, from Messages or the share sheet. Deleting the app deletes the copy on the device.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising services. It goes online only through Apple's own services: the App Store, to check whether you have an active subscription to the premium features, and iCloud for the backup. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple sends us anonymous crash reports; you can turn that off under Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Subscriptions

Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: dog-grooming@curiouscrane.dev.
