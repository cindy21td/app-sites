Groomer’s Dog Grooming Book

Groom Map of Blade and Comb

See how you groomed this dog last time, drawn on the dog, before you pick up the clippers. Groomer’s Book puts the dog on your table as a groom map: body, head, ears, tail, legs, feet and sanitary, each marked with the blade, comb attachment or scissor work you used. Last visit's map sits beside today's, and any area you change says what it was.

Made for solo groomers who work from a van, a home salon or a rented booth. It is not a booking calendar or a salon suite: no online booking, card reader or texting credits. It is the card file you check between dogs.

THE TABLE
• Tap an area, pick the tool from your own case, add a short note
• Same as last time copies the whole map in one tap
• Three outlines by build: square (poodles, doodles, bichons), long and low, deep-chested
• Start and Finish record how long the dog took

THE BOOK AND THE DAY
• Owners with any number of dogs, the gate code, alerts such as bites or matting
• Overdue, due this week and booked, from each dog's own rebook interval
• Today's stops in the order you choose, with a finish time worked out from each dog's own grooms
• Call, text or open directions to the owner from their card

THE BLADE CASE
• The blades and combs you carry come first in every picker
• Each blade counts its grooms since it was last sharpened
• A blade out for sharpening stays listed but can't be picked

CERTIFICATES
• The rabies certificate as printed: date given, 1-year or 3-year, expiry, vet
• The days left to expiry, and a note when a dog goes on the table with no certificate on file
• Groomer’s Book records what the certificate says. It is not veterinary advice, and it does not decide vaccination status or what your state or county requires.

GROOMER’S BOOK PRO
• Unlimited dogs (the free book holds 10, with every feature above)
• A reminder on the day a dog is due back, 14 days before a certificate expires, and when a blade is due for sharpening
• Report cards for the owner: before and after photos and the groom in plain words, sent from your own Messages
• Nightly iCloud backup of the book and photos, with restore on a new iPhone

A CSV of your clients and grooms is free, so your book is always yours to take with you.

Your client book is stored on this iPhone and in your own iCloud backup. Groomer’s Book sends nothing to the developer. There is no account to make and nothing to sign in to.

Groomer’s Book Pro costs $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year, and the first month of either plan is free. When the free month ends, your Apple ID account is charged for the plan you picked, and the plan renews by itself each month or year unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the current period ends. To cancel or switch plans, open Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions. Bought it before on another iPhone? Restore purchases is on the plans page and in the book's Settings.
