Groomer’s Book: Dog Grooming

Client Log, Rebook & Rabies

Know what you used on a dog last time before it's on the table. Groomer’s Book keeps a groom card for every visit: the blade on the body, the comb on the legs, how the face was scissored, what it cost and what the owner wants next time.

Made for mobile groomers, home-salon groomers and booth renters who work alone. See who is due back this week, and whether each dog's rabies is current, without opening a salon system.

Repeat last groom copies the previous card, so today's notes take seconds with wet hands. Your client book stays on your iPhone and works with no signal in the van.

GROOM CARDS
• One card per visit: service, a blade, comb attachment or scissor note for body, head, ears, tail, legs, feet and sanitary, plus shampoo, behavior 1–5, price and notes
• Blade sizes with the cut length from the Andis blade chart (7FC · 1/8″ · 3.2 mm), attachment combs in inches and mm
• The blades and combs in your case at the top of every picker
• Every visit kept per dog, newest first, so you can see how the haircut changed

REBOOK
• Overdue, Due this week and Booked, with days late
• A rebook interval for each dog, 1 to 16 weeks
• The next appointment, if it's already in your book

RABIES
• Date given, 1-year or 3-year label, first shot or booster, certificate expiry and vet
• Status from the NASPHV rabies compendium: not yet immune for 28 days after a first shot, current, expiring soon or expired
• A warning when you start a groom card for a dog with no record or an expired one

CLIENTS
• Owners with any number of dogs, plus phone, address, vet and notes such as the gate code
• Alerts on the dog: bites, matting, senior or health, muzzle, dog-aggressive
• Call, text or get directions to the owner in one tap
• Search by dog or owner

Every feature above is free for up to 10 dogs.

GROOMER PRO
• Unlimited dogs and owners
• Reminders on the day a dog is due back and 14 days before its rabies runs out
• iCloud backup of your client book, with restore on a new iPhone
• CSV of clients and groom cards, and a PDF groom history for one dog to hand to a vet or another groomer

Groomer’s Book is not a booking or payments system: no online booking, card reader or texting credits. It is the client card file, rebook list and vaccine record you check between dogs. Rabies status follows the dates you enter; your state or county may require more.

Groomer Pro is an auto-renewing subscription at $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year. Both start with a 7-day free trial, and payment is charged to your Apple ID account when the trial ends. The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it any time in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.
