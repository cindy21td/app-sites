Privacy policy

Hallwatch: Sleep Training

Hallwatch: Sleep Training works entirely on your device. It has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Data on your device

Your programs and nights (lights-out, check-in and asleep times, an optional first name) are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. Hallwatch has no account, no iCloud sync, no analytics, no ads and no microphone access. The week leaves the phone only as an image or CSV file you choose to share (Pro).

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising services, and it only goes online to talk to the App Store about your purchase.

Purchases

The paid upgrade is a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. Payments are handled by Apple and we never see your payment details. Restore the purchase on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
