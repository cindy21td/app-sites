Dim Sum Sort: Dumpling Puzzle

Cozy Steamer Stacking Game

Help Bao, the apprentice server, sort the bamboo steamers at a busy teahouse. Lift steamers onto your stack and set them down on the cart that serves each dish. Only the steamer nearest the aisle can be lifted, and your stack is last on, first off.

200 handcrafted orders across one day of service, from morning yum cha to the lantern supper, each with a solver-verified target move count. No timers, no fail states, free unlimited undo. Plays offline with no ads and no accounts.

The first 80 orders are free; one purchase unlocks the full menu.
