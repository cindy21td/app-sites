Shortfield Minesweeper

No ads. Fewer clicks next time

Clear a field, and the board stays on screen. Your clicks are counted, and a shorter route across the same mines is drawn over it: numbered purple circles joined by straight legs, like a course on an orienteering map. "26 → 12" means you used 26 clicks and this route takes 12. Tap any circle to step through it and see which cells it opens, where it flags and where one chord clears what took you five taps.

The next board, you play differently.

THE BOARD
• Beginner 9×9, Intermediate 16×16 and Expert 30×16 (upright 16×30 on a phone), plus any custom size from 5 to 40 cells a side.
• Every board can be finished by logic from the first click, which always opens a space. Switch on Classic random in Settings for the original's luck; the board then says it may need a guess.
• Tap to open, hold to flag, tap a finished number to chord. A ring fills around your finger while you hold, and the flag lands the moment it closes, so a late lift never opens the cell. Slide off the board to cancel. Hold time is adjustable from 250 to 600 ms, and a flag button is there if you prefer one.
• Hint proves the next safe cell in one sentence and outlines the numbers that prove it.
• Today's field: one Intermediate board a day, the same for everyone, with a code to send a friend. No streaks, no calendar.

SHORTFIELD PRO (one purchase)
• Field Book: every board you clear, kept with your clicks and the route, to step through or play again.
• Speed sheet: your fastest time, 3BV/s, IOE and your clicks over the route, as a trend for each size.
• Board codes: send any field to a friend and compare routes.

The free game is the whole game, and nothing in it is an advert. Pro adds the record, not a way around ads.

The route is a short way, found after you clear the board: it never uses more clicks than 3BV, and it is not always the shortest. Timer on (you can hide it), no levels, no stars, no hearts.

Pro is a one-time purchase of $2.99 that you keep: no subscription, nothing renews. To get it back on another iPhone, use Restore in Settings.
