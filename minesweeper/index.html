Shortfield Minesweeper

Fewer clicks next time. No ads

Clear a field, and the board stays on screen. A route across the same mines is drawn over it, shorter than yours whenever the field allows: purple circles joined by straight legs, like a course on an orienteering map. Above the board, one big number: the route's clicks, with two bars on one scale under it, the route's against yours (8 against 22). Hold the board and slide along the route to replay it step by step: the number follows your finger ("6 of 8 · Open D1"), and you see which cells each step opens, where it flags and where one chord clears what took you five taps. Hold still and your own clicks lie over it, with a cross on each click that did nothing. Shortfield opens on your last cleared board, so the route is the first thing you see.

The next board, you play differently.

THE BOARD
• Beginner 9×9, Intermediate 16×16 and Expert 30×16 (upright 16×30 on a phone), plus any custom size from 5 to 40 cells a side.
• Every board can be finished by logic from the first click, which always opens a space. Switch on Classic random in Settings for the original's luck; the board then says it may need a guess.
• Tap to open, hold to flag, tap a finished number to chord. A ring fills around your finger while you hold, and the flag lands the moment it closes, so a late lift never opens the cell. Slide off the board to cancel. Hold time is adjustable from 250 to 600 ms, and a flag button is there if you prefer one.
• Hint proves the next safe cell in one line under the board and outlines the number that proves it.
• Today's field: one Intermediate board a day, the same for everyone, with a code to send a friend. No streaks, no calendar.

SHORTFIELD PRO (one purchase)
• Field Book: every board you clear, one to a page with its route, swiped back through time; open one to step through it or play it again. Without Pro it shows your latest cleared board.
• Speed sheet: your fastest time, 3BV/s, IOE, boards cleared of boards played and your clicks over the route, as a trend for each size.
• Board codes: send any field to a friend and compare routes.

The free game is the whole game, and nothing in it is an advert. Pro adds the record, not a way around ads.

The route is a short way, found after you clear the board: it never uses more clicks than 3BV, and it is not always the shortest. While you play, mines left is the one number; tap it for clicks and time, no levels, no stars, no hearts.

Pro is a one-time purchase of $2.99 that you keep: no subscription, nothing renews. To get it back on another iPhone, use Restore in Settings.
