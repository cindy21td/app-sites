Endcard Archery Scorecard

How Endcard works

Endcard is a scorecard you fill in at the target. Open it and you are on the card: there is no sign-up and no start button.

Your first card

  • On first launch, Endcard asks What do you shoot most? Pick your division (Recurve, Compound or Barebow) and a round. The blank card opens with a pad under it.
  • The pad only shows the values your target face can score. On a three-spot 18 m face that is 10 to 6 and M.
  • To shoot a different round later, use ··· › Pick a round. Custom round lets you set its name, distance, arrow count, end size and the lowest ring the keys go down to.

Scoring an end

  • Tap each arrow as you call it. Every tap is saved straight away.
  • The end sorts itself high to low, like a paper card, and closes when it is full. There is no Next End key.
  • Undo takes back the last arrow, or the last end once it has closed. The button says what it will take back, for example Undo end 6.
  • To correct an older arrow, use ··· › Fix an arrow, or press the arrow on the card. The card stays in view while you change the value.
  • Stopping before the last end? ··· › Finish round early closes the card with the score you shot.

The Pace Ladder

The column down the right edge of the card is the Pace Ladder. It compares this card with the one you are chasing, end by end.

  • The dashed line in the middle is the chased card. After each end the line steps left if you are behind it and right if you are ahead, by your running margin.
  • Behind is shaded in pencil hatching, ahead is gold, and each end prints its plus or minus next to it. The number at the top of the ladder is where the round stands now.
  • Your first card of a round has no earlier card to chase. Set a target score for it (free on a round's first card) and the ladder draws from the first end. From your second card of the same round, the ladder chases your last card of that round automatically.
  • Drag down the ladder to read it end by end.
  • Choose what to chase under ··· › Chase: Last card, Best card, A score you set, or Nothing. After a round's first card, Best card and a score you set are part of Premium. ··· › Set a target score asks for a score, for example 600, and the ladder chases that pace.
  • Prefer not to see your score mid-round? ··· › Hide pace removes the ladder and gives its width to the arrows. Settings › Show pace during a round sets the default.

After the round

  • When the last end closes, the card shows your total and average arrow. A new personal best is crossed in gold.
  • Score another starts the same round again. With Premium, Share card posts an image of the whole card and its ladder.
  • Cards (top left) lists every card you have shot, each with its total and a small ladder. Tap one to see its ends, stats and notes.

Your records

  • Settings › Export CSV, then Share CSV of every card, exports all your cards. Export stays free, with or without Premium.
  • With iCloud on, cards back up to your own iCloud. Settings › Restore from iCloud brings them back on a new phone. It replaces the cards on that phone with the backup.

Premium

Premium adds the best-card chase, targets on any card, the Bow and sight book (sight marks per distance, printed on the card), Season (scores over the season and which ends you fade in), the share card and the PDF scorecard. It is a monthly or yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial. Restore purchases is on the Premium screen and in Settings.
