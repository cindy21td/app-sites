Endcard: Archery Score Tracker

Scoresheet & Personal Best Log

Score each end at the target and see, while you shoot, how far this card is ahead of or behind your personal best. Endcard is a practice and league scoresheet for target archers: recurve, compound and barebow.

Open it and you are on the card. There is no sign-up and no start button: pick your division and round once, then tap each arrow as you call it. Every tap is saved. The end sorts itself high to low like a paper card and closes when it is full, so there is no Next End key to forget.

THE PACE LADDER
Down the right edge of the card, a line steps left or right after each end by your running margin on the card you are chasing. Behind is shaded, ahead is gold, and each end prints its plus or minus beside it. You know where the round stands before you pull.

BUILT FOR THE SHOOTING LINE
- Keys are big, flush to the screen edge, and only show the values the face can score, so a gloved thumb hits the right one.
- Undo is one tap and names what it takes back. Fix an arrow from any closed end without leaving the card.
- Indoor and outdoor rounds at 18 m and every outdoor distance, single-spot and three-spot faces, and custom rounds with your own arrow count and keys.
- Works in airplane mode. Your cards stay on your iPhone, with optional backup to your own iCloud.

FREE, FOR GOOD
Score unlimited rounds, keep every card in your archery journal, see your best per round and division, and chase your last card. Export a CSV of every card at any time, including after a subscription ends.

PREMIUM
- Chase your best card or a target you set, such as 600, on the card and in the Live Activity on your Lock Screen.
- Bow and sight book: bows, arrows and sight marks per distance, with today’s mark printed in the card header and bests split by bow.
- Season view: your round scores over time, the ends where you tend to fade, and a PDF scorecard to share or file.

Endcard is a practice and league record, not an official tournament score keeper.
