Film Log: Analog Roll Tracker

35mm & 120 Exposure Notes

Write down the exposure between frames and get the camera back to your eye. Film Log keeps a frame-by-frame record of every roll you shoot on 35mm and 120: aperture, shutter, lens and the place you stood.

Tap Log frame 13 and frame 12's aperture, shutter and lens are already set. Change only what changed and tap once. Rolls, frames and development notes are free with no roll limit, and nothing for sale ever interrupts a roll halfway through.

FREE
• One card per camera with the film in it: stock, box ISO, rated EI and the push or pull in stops, the frame counter, frames left and days in camera
• Aperture and shutter wheels marked like your lens ring and speed dial, plus typed values such as f/1.7 or 1/90
• The place name on each frame from your iPhone's location, read only while you log
• Skip a blank frame, delete with undo, and log a missed number into its gap later
• A ruled film log card for each roll, with a note for the whole roll
• A development record: lab or self, developer, dilution, temperature, time and agitation, dates sent and back
• Rolls from loaded to scanned, searchable by stock, camera or note
• Your cameras and lenses: SLR, rangefinder, 35mm, and 120 from 6×4.5 to 6×9
• Backup to your own iCloud. No account and no ads

PREMIUM
• A PDF roll sheet to print and file with the negatives, and a CSV of one roll or every frame
• A film stash with expiry dates: load a roll straight from the box in the fridge and see which stock is about to expire
• Reminders to pick up scans from the lab and to finish a roll that has sat in the camera for weeks

Film Log is a notebook for film photography, made for people who shoot manual cameras, push HP5 to 1600, and want to know which frame was which when the negatives come back from the darkroom or the lab. It records what you set; it does not meter light or work out development times.

Premium is an auto-renewing subscription at $2.99 a month or $19.99 a year. Both start with a 7-day free trial, and payment is charged to your Apple ID account when the trial ends. The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it any time in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.
