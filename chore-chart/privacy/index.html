Privacy policy

Read the Fridge Chore Chart

Read the Fridge Chore Chart works on your device and your own iCloud. It has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Data on your device

The children's first names or nicknames, the chores, the ticks and, if you keep them, small crops of each ticked box are stored on your iPhone and synced through your own iCloud account (Apple CloudKit). The photo of the sheet is read on the phone and discarded; it is never stored or sent. If you share the household with another grown-up, the person you invite through Apple's iCloud sharing sees and edits the same sheets; nothing goes to the developer. Children do not use the app. No account, no analytics, no ads.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising services, and it only goes online through Apple's own services: iCloud to sync your children's first names, chores and ticks and the App Store for purchases.

Purchases

The paid features are an auto-renewing subscription bought through Apple. Payments are handled by Apple and we never see your payment details. The app asks the App Store on your device whether the subscription is active; no purchase data is sent to us. Manage or cancel it in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions, and restore it on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
