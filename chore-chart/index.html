Read the Fridge Chore Chart

Paper reward chart & allowance

Keep the chore chart on paper, where the children already look, and stop doing its sums. Print the week, the kids tick their boxes on the fridge all week, and on Sunday night you photograph the sheet: the app reads every box back, counts each child's ticks and works out the allowance.

READ THE SHEET BACK
The printed week carries four corner squares and a small code. Point the camera at it and each box on the paper lands on the same box in the app: ticked, empty, or dashed when a mark is too faint to call. Tap a dashed box to decide it. Nothing to type for the boxes that were clear.

THE WEEK AS IT PRINTS
One band per child, a row per job, seven boxes. Days a job isn't on print shaded. A short line under each day shows how many minutes of jobs each child has, so you can see one at 23 minutes and another at 8 and move a row. Nothing is assigned for you.

SETTLE ON SUNDAY
Each child's count ("Maya 15 of 17"), their ticks for the reward shelf ("Pick Friday's movie: 15 ticks"), and a fresh sheet ready to print for next week.

CHILDREN DON'T NEED THE APP
No logins, no passcodes, no second device. The children use the paper; the app is for the grown-ups. The photo of the sheet is never kept.

PRO
- A second grown-up settles the same sheet, shared through iCloud: no account to make
- Allowance worked out to the cent, with what's owed and what's paid
- Every old sheet with the children's own ticks, not just this week and last

Every child, every job, printing and reading back are free.
