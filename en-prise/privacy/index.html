Privacy policy

En Prise Safety Check

En Prise Safety Check works entirely on your device. It has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Data on your device

Your games, their analysis and your safety-check history are stored on your iPhone only. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Data we collect

None collected by us. The app has no analytics, crash reporting or advertising services and no server of ours. When you type your chess.com username, the app sends that username to chess.com's public archive (api.chess.com) to fetch your public games; it is not sent anywhere else and we do not store it on any server. Purchases go through the App Store.

Purchases

The paid features are an auto-renewing subscription bought through Apple. Payments are handled by Apple and we never see your payment details. The app asks the App Store on your device whether the subscription is active; no purchase data is sent to us. Manage or cancel it in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions, and restore it on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
