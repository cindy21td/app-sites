En Prise Safety Check

Chess analysis & threat drill

Before you move, look. En Prise sets up the position from your own chess.com game one move before your mistake, and asks you to mark every check, capture and threat on the board, for both sides. Only when you tap Done looking does it draw what was there: the ones you found, the ones you missed, and any threat you walked past in red. Then you play your move.

That look is the habit club players are told to build and rarely practise. En Prise turns the positions where you actually went wrong into the drill.

THE SAFETY CHECK
Drag from a piece to a square to mark a move; tap a mark to take it back. The answer comes from move generation, not an opinion: every legal check and capture for both sides, and the threats among them, a mate in one or a capture that wins material once the exchanges are counted. The count sits under the board, "1 of 2 threats", and the arrows you missed stay drawn while you choose your move. Checks come back on a spaced schedule: a clean look waits longer, a missed threat returns tomorrow.

YOUR GAMES, READ ON YOUR PHONE
Type your chess.com username. No password and no account: your games are public, and En Prise reads them from chess.com's public archive. Rapid, blitz and daily count; bullet is off unless you turn it on. The analysis runs on the iPhone with En Prise's own engine, offline once the games are in, and new games are read while the phone charges.

MISTAKES NAMED ONLY WHEN SURE
A game is labelled only with tags that proved at least 95% accurate against a strong engine on hundreds of real games. Missed mate in one or two clears that bar today and is shown on your games and the leak board. Hung pieces, ignored threats and big drops did not clear it yet, so they are held back and marked as held back rather than guessed. Their positions still become safety checks, because the check itself is exact.

THE SCORESHEET, THE LEAK BOARD AND THE WEEK
Every game replays on the board with its scoresheet beside it. The leak board draws the squares where your missed mates were, with the piece that would have played them, and the week view sets this week's games, missed mates and cleared checks against last week's.

EN PRISE PRO
Free: your newest 10 games, your top leak, and 3 safety checks a day.
Pro adds every game you have played and every new one, the whole leak board, safety checks without a daily stop, and the rating roadmap.
Pro costs $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year and begins with 7 free days. The first charge to your Apple Account comes when those days end, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before; after that Pro renews automatically each month or year until you cancel it in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Pro bought on your other iPhone or iPad comes back with Restore on the Pro page.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No sign-in, no analytics, no ads. The only thing En Prise sends anywhere is the username you type, to chess.com's public archive, to fetch your games. The games, the analysis and your safety-check history stay on this iPhone.
