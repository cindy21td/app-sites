Glaze Log: Pottery Tracker

Ceramics, Cone & Kiln Notes

Remember which glaze went over which. Glaze Log keeps every test tile and pot as the stack you actually brushed on: each glaze in order, its coats, the clay body and the cone.

Tap the jars on your shelf in the order they go on, and the layer section draws itself. When the kiln opens, Unload walks you through what came out, one test at a time: take a photo, then mark it Keep, Adjust or Never.

Your records stay yours. Glaze Log copies your tests and photos to your own iCloud, with no account with us, and the full export works whether or not you subscribe.

FREE
• Unlimited tests, with every layer, its coats and how it went on
• A shelf for commercial jars and your own mixes
• Unload: a photo and a verdict for each test out of the kiln
• The Board: every fired result, by glaze, clay and cone
• iCloud copy, restore and full export

PRO
• Over/Under: pick two glazes and see both orders on every clay body, including the glaze combinations you've never tried. Test this starts the stack for you
• Recipe bench: base and additions, saved versions, batch sheets in g, oz or lb, and line blends between two glazes
• Test sheets: a PDF for the studio wall or your class, and a share card for one result

Glaze Log is for potters who glaze in small batches: community studio members, class students and home studios. It's a pottery journal for glaze results and test tiles, not a piece-by-piece stage tracker, and it says nothing about food safety or materials.

If your subscription ends, every test, photo, recipe and past batch sheet stays readable and in the export. Only the Over/Under grid, new recipes and new PDFs lock.

Pro is an auto-renewing subscription at $3.99 a month or $24.99 a year. Both start with a 7-day free trial, and payment is charged to your Apple ID account when the trial ends. The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it any time in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.
