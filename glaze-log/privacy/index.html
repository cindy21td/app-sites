Privacy policy

Glaze Test Tiles for Pottery

Glaze Test Tiles for Pottery works on your device and your own iCloud. It has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Data on your device

Your records and photos and settings are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, the app also keeps a copy in your own iCloud account so they can be restored. We cannot see either copy. Deleting the app removes the copy on the device; the iCloud copy stays with your Apple ID so reinstalling restores your records and photos.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising services, and it only goes online through Apple's own services: iCloud to keep the copy of your records and photos and the App Store for purchases.

Purchases

The paid features are an auto-renewing subscription bought through Apple. Payments are handled by Apple and we never see your payment details. The app asks the App Store on your device whether the subscription is active; no purchase data is sent to us. Manage or cancel it in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions, and restore it on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
