Privacy policy

Lastgood: Widget Scripts

Lastgood: Widget Scripts is built to work on your device. It has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Data you enter

Your scripts and their Git history stay in the folder you choose (on your iPhone, in iCloud Drive, or in another app's folder) and on the Git remotes you add; Lastgood has no server and no account. A personal access token for a remote is kept in your iPhone's Keychain and sent only to that remote. The shelf (which commit each widget runs and its test results) is kept on your iPhone. A script reaches only the hosts you allow for it, and Lastgood sends nothing to us.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising services and has no server of ours. It goes online only for what you set up: the Git remotes you add, the hosts you allow a script to reach, iCloud Drive if your folder is there, and the App Store to check whether you have an active subscription.

Subscriptions

Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
