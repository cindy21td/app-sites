Privacy policy

Daughter Tongue: Conlang Lab

Daughter Tongue: Conlang Lab works entirely on your device. It has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Data on your device

Your languages (their sounds, words, sound laws and notes) are stored on your iPhone only. CSV and PDF files leave the device only when you share them. Nothing goes to the developer. The app has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting SDK and no ads.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising services, and it only goes online to talk to the App Store about your purchase.

Purchases

The paid features are an auto-renewing subscription bought through Apple. Payments are handled by Apple and we never see your payment details. The app asks the App Store on your device whether the subscription is active; no purchase data is sent to us. Manage or cancel it in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions, and restore it on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
