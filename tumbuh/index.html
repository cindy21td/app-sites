Tumbuh: Color Mood Diary

Year in Pixels & Feelings

Tumbuh is a daily journal that starts with a colour instead of a number or a smiley face.
Each day you pick the one of 25 palettes that matches how it felt, tag up to three feelings, and write as much or as little as you like.
No account, no ads, and every feature works in airplane mode. The app is written in Bahasa Indonesia.

A YEAR AS CLOTH
Every day you log becomes a dyed thread. Twelve months are woven into one cloth that nobody else will have. Save it or share it as an image.

MONTHLY KALEIDOSKOP
A colour calendar for the month, plus a share card whose colours come from the palette you picked most.

YOUR WEEK
A seven-day summary, weekdays compared with weekends, your most frequent feelings, and one reflection question each week that opens straight into the writing page.

WHICH ONE ARE YOU?
Eight quick questions place you as Si Pemikir (the thinker), Si Penjaga (the keeper), Si Pejuang (the fighter) or Si Pengamat (the observer). Weekly questions and lines follow your type.

ACTUALLY PRIVATE
There is no account and no Tumbuh server. Your journal lives on your phone and, if you switch it on, in your own iCloud so it survives a new phone. You can also export and import a single backup file.

REMINDER
One daily reminder at the time you choose, scheduled on the device.

Tumbuh is a personal journal for getting to know yourself. It is not a health service and does not replace professional support.
