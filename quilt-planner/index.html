Bedfall Quilt Fit for Your Bed

Quilters' AR measure, yardage

Start a bed quilt at the bed. Hold the phone over the mattress, tap its four corners, and Bedfall reads the width and length; tap the floor and it has the depth too. Say how far the quilt should hang, and it sizes itself to whole blocks: "Six across gives a 10 inch drop. Seven gives 16."

Then step back. On the bed lays the planned quilt over your own mattress in the camera at true size, with the drop marked at the side and the foot, so you see it hang before a yard is bought. No camera handy, or measuring from a tape? Type the size and see it drawn to scale instead.

The home screen is that quilt drawn on the bed from above: the mattress edge dashed, the drops hatched and dimensioned, a red hem to pull. Drag it and the blocks refit; pick 5, 6 or 7 across if you'd rather tap.

LAY OUT THE TOP
• 24 traditional blocks: nine patch, Ohio star, churn dash, log cabin, flying geese, sawtooth star and more
• Tap a patch to change that patch in every block; long-press a block to turn it a quarter
• Sashing with or without cornerstones, up to three borders

THEN THE YARDAGE
• Yards to buy for each fabric, rounded up to the eighth
• A cut list set like the back of a pattern: "Cut 7 strips 5¼″ × WOF → 48 squares", ticked off at the mat
• Binding and backing worked out from the finished size, with shrinkage per fabric for prewashers

Each quilt is its own .bedfall file in Files, to keep, duplicate or AirDrop to a friend in your guild.

Your first quilt is complete and free: the bed fit, the bed view, the layout, the yardage and the cut list. Bedfall Pro adds more quilts, photos of your own fabric drawn at their real scale, and the printed pattern.

Bedfall Pro is a one-time purchase that covers every quilt you plan after the first: there is nothing to renew or cancel. Bought it on another iPhone? Settings › Restore purchase brings it back on the same Apple Account.
