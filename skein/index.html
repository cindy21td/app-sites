Skein: Story Planner

Novel Writing, Scene Timeline

Skein is a story planner for novelists. Jot an idea, connect it to the characters and places it involves, put the scenes in order, and read the result as a manuscript.

It works offline, keeps your project on your iPhone and iPad, and syncs through your own iCloud. No account, no subscription, no AI.

LOOSE IDEAS FIRST
Tap Note and write. The idea waits in your inbox, unsorted, for as long as you like. When you know what it is, promote it to the graph.

A MAP OF YOUR STORY
Scenes, characters and places sit on one canvas and pull together where you connect them. Drag a node to pin it, pinch to zoom, long-press to open it. Label connections in your own words: "betrays", "grew up here", anything.

SCENES ON INDEX CARDS
The timeline lays your scenes out as cards. Drag one by its grip to move it. Each card shows who is in the scene and how many words it has.

FOLLOW A CHARACTER
Pick a character or place and Skein draws their thread through the timeline: a knot on every scene they are in, a dashed line where they are offstage. You can see at a glance where someone drops out of the story.

THE MANUSCRIPT, ASSEMBLED
Scene text is set in order as a book page, with a word count per scene and in total. Export to Word (.docx) or Markdown and send it anywhere from the share sheet.

YOURS, ON YOUR DEVICES
Everything is stored on your device. With iCloud on, your iPhone and iPad stay in step, and edits are combined scene by scene, so writing done on one is never overwritten by the other. Back up to a file whenever you want.

Search everything with ⌘K, capture with ⌘J on an iPad keyboard. Paper and Ink themes.

Free to plan. Skein Pro, a one-time purchase, adds Word and Markdown export.
