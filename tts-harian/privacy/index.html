Privacy policy

Petak Pagi · Teka Teki Silang

Petak Pagi · Teka Teki Silang works on your device and your own iCloud. It has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Data on your device

Your editions and progress and settings are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, the app also keeps a copy in your own iCloud account so your other iPhones stay in sync. We cannot see either copy. Deleting the app removes the copy on the device; the iCloud copy stays with your Apple ID so reinstalling restores your editions and progress.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising services, and it only goes online through Apple's own services: iCloud to sync your editions and progress and the App Store for purchases.

To get new and corrected crossword editions, the app downloads one public file (editions.json) from this website when it opens. The request is a plain download: it carries no account, device or advertising identifier, no cookies and no personal data; it is the same file for every reader. If the download fails, the app uses the editions it shipped with.

Purchases

The paid upgrade is a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. Payments are handled by Apple and we never see your payment details. Restore the purchase on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
