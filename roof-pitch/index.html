Roof Pitch Calculator: Gauge

Slope Level, Rafter & Squares

Set the phone on a rafter, the sheathing or the shingles and read the roof pitch: a big x/12 number you can see from a ladder, with the angle in degrees and the grade in percent beneath it. Tap Hold to keep the reading, Zero it on a known-level surface, and it reads the same lengthwise or across the slope.

Every number names its basis: rafter-table factors as on the framing square, and the IRC 2024 Chapter 9 minimum slopes for each covering.

FREE, NO LIMITS
• Pitch gauge: x/12 to a tenth, degrees, percent, with Hold and Zero
• Converter: enter rise per 12, degrees, percent, or a measured rise and run, and get all the others, plus the slope factor and the common and hip lengths per foot of run
• Common rafter length: span or run, ridge board and overhang in; line length, tail, total and ridge rise out in feet, inches and eighths (or sixteenths)
• Code check: which coverings the IRC allows at this pitch (asphalt shingles, tile, metal, slate, wood shakes, roll roofing), when double underlayment is required, and the OSHA low-slope or steep roof label

PRO, ONE-TIME PURCHASE
• Squares and bundles: footprint, overhang and pitch to roof area, squares and the bundles to order, with your waste % for gable or hip
• Hip, valley and jack rafters: hip length and tail, the jack common difference at 16" or 24" on center, and the full jack list
• Jobs: save any reading, rafter or takeoff to a named job with notes, and send the office or the supply house a PDF or CSV

Built for roofers, framers and carpenters on a pitched deck: one hand, big numbers, readable in sun, and your last inputs are still there next time.
