Sauna Stove Times · Stoveside

Heat-up planner for the heater

When to light the sauna stove, worked out from your own stove's past heat-ups and the thermometer on the wall: for a barrel or cabin sauna with a wood stove, or an electric heater without wifi. No connected heater, no sensor, no watch.

It reads 5° in the barrel and you want to sit at seven: light at 5:30.

Each heat-up is two readings: what the dial said when you lit it, and when it got to temperature. From those Stoveside learns how many minutes this stove takes per degree, and turns tonight's reading, your target and the time you want to sit into a lighting time. Drag the green mark round the dial to change the heat; drag the sit mark along the evening strip under it, and the light-by time moves with you.

TONIGHT
- The light-by time, large enough to read at the back door
- The stove dial: tonight's reading and your target, temperature only
- The evening strip: now, light, sit, with the heat-up span between them
- Tap Lit when the match goes in; the heat-up shows on your Lock Screen with the time it should be ready
- Tap At temperature when the dial gets there; that heat-up sharpens the next answer
- "Lit the sauna" from Shortcuts, Siri or the Action button, for hands full of firewood

AFTER
- The round sheet: heat, cool-down and rest per round, timed from the phone while it stays outside the hot room, or typed afterwards; bench temperature and ladles of löyly
- This week's minutes in the heat and the last 30 days of evenings
- Every heat-up on one chart: minutes against degrees of rise
- °C or °F; works for wood stoves and electric heaters

STOVESIDE PLUS
- The fitted heat-up line with its range once you have five heat-ups that differ enough: "light at 5:35, ± 5 min"
- A reminder at the light-by time
- More than one sauna, and every heat-up and evening kept

Stoveside gives a lighting time from your own readings. It has no temperature or time limits and gives no health advice: follow your heater maker's instructions. No account; your heat-ups stay on your phone as four files you can send yourself.
