Aetherbound: The Codex

Text RPG, idle MMORPG & quests

Your champion fights. You give the orders.

Aetherbound is a text RPG for people who like LitRPG and idle games but play in five-minute gaps. Write your champion's battle orders as plain sentences ("If the foe has no Bleed, Rend"), test them against the next boss with a hundred practice bouts, then seal the order and send it out. While you're away it hunts, gathers and gets into trouble. When you come back, the field report tells you what happened, line by line, and the spoils come home.

A System you didn't ask for
A blue box in the corner of your sight announces level-ups, ranks your arts, hands out titles and comments on your choices. It has done this too many times. It will tell you.

What you do
• Send your champion out with a swipe of a wax seal, or call it home early
• Read field reports and choose what to do when something happens on the road home
• Spend attribute points in the Status Window, or let the System advise
• Write gambits: ordered rules your champion checks every turn, with a trial card showing your odds before and after
• Break the walls: solo bosses and party bosses that open the next region
• Wear, upgrade, meld and salvage 73 pieces of gear across five sets
• Help ten people of the broken world with their errands
• Raise up to three champions, join a guild, chase the world boss, delve the weekly Rift

How it starts
Pick a champion, name it, tap Begin. No email needed. The System walks you through your first expedition on the real screens, then gets out of the way.

The world is online: your champion keeps adventuring on the server while the app is closed. No gacha, no loot boxes, and nothing for sale makes a champion stronger. Gear is only ever earned.
