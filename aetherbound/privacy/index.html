Privacy policy

Aetherbound: The Codex

Aetherbound: The Codex is an online game: your champion keeps playing on our server while the app is closed, so the game needs an account. It has no ads, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Your account

  • Begin without an email. Tapping Begin creates an account with an email address and password the app makes up and keeps on your phone. We learn nothing about you from it.
  • Or with an email. If you choose to begin with your own email address (to play on more than one phone), we store that address and a salted, hashed password (we never store the password itself). We use the address only to sign you in; we do not send marketing email.
  • The name you choose for your champion (and a guild name, if you found one) is shown to other players on leaderboards and in guilds. Don't use your real name if you'd rather not.

Game data on our server

To run the game, our server stores what happens in it: your champions, their gear and orders, expeditions and their results, quests, the Chronicle, guild membership, leaderboard standings and in-game currency. It is linked to your game account and used only to run the game and balance it. The server runs on Render with a Postgres database (Neon); both act as our processors. Our request logs record which game action was called and how long it took, not your IP address; the hosting provider keeps its own standard connection logs.

Data on your phone

The app keeps your sign-in tokens, the made-up account details if you began without an email, and small settings (such as where you are in the first quest) on your phone. Reminders that your champion is home are scheduled on the phone itself; no push token is sent to us.

Deleting your account

In the app, tap your initial in the top corner, then Delete this account. That deletes the account, its sign-in, your champions, gear, runs, Chronicle and progress from our server straight away. Shared totals (a world boss's damage, a guild's existence) keep their numbers without your name. You can also ask by email below.

Purchases and ratings

The iPhone app has no in-app purchases. If the app asks you to rate it, that is Apple's own prompt; we see only the public rating.

We do not sell data, share it with data brokers, or use it for advertising.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
