Deep Signal: Space Horror

Survival Text Adventure RPG

You wake up in your cryo pod. The ship's AI says good morning and tells you that you are almost home. She is lying, and something in the corridors has just heard you breathe.

Deep Signal is a survival horror text adventure aboard the research cruiser Erebus-VII. Explore four decks, piece together what happened from the crew's contradictory logs, decide how much to trust ALIS, and stay quiet: the thing in the corridors hunts by sound and by light. Every time you die, you wake again, and the ship remembers.

11 endings. Fully offline: no account, no ads, no tracking.
