Deep Signal: Space Horror

Survival Text Adventure RPG

The lid lifts. Frost slides down the glass. A warm voice tells you good morning, that you're nearly home, that you're safe.

Then you step into the corridor, and at the far bend something turns its head toward the sound of your breathing.

Deep Signal is a survival horror text adventure set aboard the research cruiser Erebus-VII. Eleven crew went into cryo. You're the only one who came out. Search four decks, read what the crew left behind, and decide how far to trust ALIS, the ship's AI. She is very kind to you. She also controls the doors.

THE LISTENER
It makes no sound. It walks the corridors in straight lines and it hunts by noise and by light. Drop a mug and it comes. Switch on your flashlight in the dark and it comes faster. Listen before you move. Hide when it's close. Count to eighteen.

SURVIVE THE SHIP
• Four decks, from the crew ring to a lander clamped to the outside of the hull
• Bulkheads slam, lights die, air leaks, gravity cuts out. No two crossings play the same
• Manage sanity, oxygen, noise and a flashlight that won't last
• Hide in lockers, crates and morgue drawers. Throw flares. Hold your breath
• Arm the reactor if you have to. The counter doesn't stop
• Examine everything. The crew hid what they knew in the details

IT REMEMBERS YOU
Die, and you wake in the pod again. There's one more scratch on the lid. Your last body is where you dropped it. Supplies are where a previous you moved them. ALIS remembers what you chose. And the thing in the corridors has started using your voice.

• 23 logs from frightened people who don't agree on what happened
• An AI with four moods, from warm to broken to silent
• 11 endings, including two true endings and three secrets
• Hallucinations that rewrite the text as your sanity slips

MADE FOR PHONES
• One-tap choices, examine chips and a quick-action bar
• Four terminal palettes, adjustable text speed and size
• Procedural sound: ship hum, the 18-second pulse, a heartbeat when it's close (play with headphones)
• Haptics on every encounter
• Fully offline. No account, no ads, no tracking.
