Privacy policy

Jajan Pasar: Merge Kue

Jajan Pasar: Merge Kue has no accounts and asks for no personal details. It shows optional ads, served by Google AdMob, which you choose to watch for a reward.

Data on your device

Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads only play when you tap a button to watch one. To show and measure them, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects:

  • Device identifiers: the advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, only if you allow tracking; the Android advertising ID on Android) and an app-scoped identifier.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Ad interactions and diagnostics: which ads were shown and tapped, app launches, and performance or crash data from the ad SDK.

Google uses this data to serve ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, only where you have agreed, personalise them. On iPhone the app asks for permission before any tracking. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland it asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice later. Your data is handled under Google's privacy policy; see also how Google uses data from apps that use its services. You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device's privacy settings.

We do not collect any of this data ourselves, and we do not sell data.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (gem packs) are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details; the app only receives a receipt so it can add what you bought.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
