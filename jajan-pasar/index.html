Jajan Pasar: Merge Kue

Cozy Indonesian Market Merge

Run a little jajan pasar stall and bring a sleepy Indonesian market street back to life.

Tap your bakul to make snacks, drag two of the same together to merge them into something better, from rice flour all the way up to a festive tumpeng, and serve your neighbours' orders for coins. Spend the coins renovating the street: new awnings, lanterns, flowers and stalls, chapter by chapter from morning market to night market.

• Merge 10 kinds of kue and 6 drinks on a woven tray
• Generators that level up too: baskets and drink carts
• 3 chapters of renovations that change the street and the time of day
• Energy refills over time, even when the app is closed
• Hand-painted art and gamelan music

Free to play. Optional ads (only when you choose to watch one) give extra energy, and gem packs are available as in-app purchases.
