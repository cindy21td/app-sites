Rabbitry Log: Rabbit Breeding

Litter, Pedigree, Herd Manager

Every doe in your rabbitry on one board, sorted by where her cycle is today: open, bred, palpate, nest box and kindle, nursing. Mark a doe bred and the palpation window, nest box day and due date follow on their own.

Each doe's card shows her ear number, how many days she's been bred and what comes next: "palpate by Tue", "nest box in today", "late, due Oct 22", "wean today". Tap Palpated, Nest box in or Kindled at the hutch and the card moves to the next row.

FREE
• The breeding board for up to 6 rabbits: does, bucks and juniors
• Bred sheet with the dates that follow: palpate day 10 to 14, nest box in on day 28, due on day 31, all adjustable in Settings
• Pair check before you breed: direct kin and any ancestor shared within three generations, with how much of each pedigree is known
• Litter records: kindled date, born alive and dead, fostered in and out, weaned count, and Keep a kit to add it to the herd with sire, dam and birth date filled in

PRO
• Unlimited does, bucks and juniors
• Reminders on palpation, nest box, due and wean days, at the hour you choose
• A three-generation pedigree PDF with ear numbers under your rabbitry's name, no app branding, ready to hand to a buyer
• CSV export of the herd and every litter, and daily iCloud backup to your own account

Built for meat rabbitries, show breeders and 4-H families. Works offline with no account to sign in to. The breeding calendar is a planning aid you set yourself, not veterinary advice.
