Privacy policy

Rabbit Breeding Rack, Pedigree

Rabbit Breeding Rack, Pedigree works on your device and your own iCloud. It has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Data on your device

Your herd, litters and pedigrees are stored on your device. With Rabbitry Log Pro and iCloud on, the app also keeps them in your own private iCloud database so your iPhone and iPad show the same herd; only your Apple ID can read that copy, and we cannot see it. You can turn this off in the app under Settings › iCloud. Deleting the app deletes the copy on the device; the iCloud copy can be removed under your iPhone's Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising services, and it only goes online through Apple's own services: iCloud to sync your records and the App Store for purchases.

Purchases

The paid features are an auto-renewing subscription bought through Apple. Payments are handled by Apple and we never see your payment details. The app asks the App Store on your device whether the subscription is active; no purchase data is sent to us. Manage or cancel it in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions, and restore it on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
