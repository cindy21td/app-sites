Warung: Mahjong Jajanan

Cozy Indonesian Tile Matching

Open your warung, fill the tray and match street food from all over Indonesia!

Tap a food tile to send it to the loyang (tray). Three of the same disappear. Clear the whole board to win, but careful: the tray only holds 7 tiles. Easy to learn, and trickier the higher the stacks get.

A FOOD TOUR OF INDONESIA
• 6 regions: Jakarta, Bandung, Yogyakarta, Padang, Manado and Bali
• 300 tested levels, every one guaranteed solvable
• 60 regional dishes, hand-drawn in the dishes they're really served in: enamel plates, rooster bowls, banana leaves, stone mortars and iced tea in a plastic bag

RELAXING, BUT A REAL PUZZLE
• Mahjong-style stacked tiles that get more challenging as you go
• Shuffle, Undo, Clear 3 and Hint power-ups for when you're stuck
• Earn 3 stars to unlock the next region
• The first 3 levels have free power-ups while you learn

PLAY ANY TIME
• Plays offline
• Daily rewards and lives that refill on their own
• Bahasa Indonesia and English

Free to play. Rewarded ads are always optional, and gems are available as in-app purchases. There's also a one-time Remove Ads pack.

Selamat makan, and happy matching!
