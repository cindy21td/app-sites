Undak Bahasa Jawa & Sunda

Ngoko, krama, loma & lemes

Pinten niki, Bu? The same question changes with the person you ask. In Undak every sentence stands on a flight of steps: you on one step, the warung owner one step above you, Grandma two steps up. Move the person and the sentence changes with them, from ngoko to krama and krama inggil in Javanese, or from loma to lemes in Sundanese.

Undak is for people who live around Javanese or Sundanese but never learned it: you moved to Semarang or Bandung for work or university, you are visiting your partner's family, or you want to greet your grandparents politely at Lebaran. Lessons are explained in Indonesian or English, your choice.

A KAMPUNG MAP, NOT A LESSON LIST
Home is a drawn kampung: the warung, the boarding house, the market, the minibus, Grandma's house. Each place opens its own lessons, and its people turn up again at the next place. The gate at the end of the lane switches between the Semarang and the Bandung kampung, each with its own progress.

PICK THE STEP BEFORE YOU ANSWER
Exercises go past translation. You place the person you are addressing on the right step, build sentences from word tiles, match pairs and fill in turns of short written dialogues. A wrong answer is shown on the steps with the right form.

BALIK LAGI (COME BACK)
A sentence you missed returns tomorrow, then after 3, 7 and 16 days, with the person you said it to. On the map they wait outside their place.

HOW TO READ IT
Every sentence has an as-read spelling, and each tricky sound gets one hint card the first time it appears. This version is text only, with no audio.

WHAT THE FIRST VERSION HAS
Semarang Javanese: 8 lessons at the warung, boarding house, market and minibus. Bandung Sundanese: 4 lessons at the warung and boarding house. The other places on the map arrive in updates.

GLASSES OF TEA
Every lesson is free to use with 5 glasses of tea. A wrong answer empties a glass, one glass refills every 2 hours, and a finished Balik lagi session refills one. Places on the map open in order.

LANGGANAN UNDAK
Unlimited glasses of tea, and any place on the map opens straight away without finishing the ones before it.
