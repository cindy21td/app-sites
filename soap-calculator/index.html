Lye Scale: Soap Calculator

Cold Process Recipe & Batch

Weigh your oils and Lye Scale gives you the lye and water to weigh, with your superfat already taken off. Cold process bars or liquid soap, NaOH, KOH or both.

Every oil names its SAP range and the standard it comes from, so you can see where the number came from. Before it shows a result, Lye Scale checks the worst case: if the low end of your oils' SAP ranges could make the bar lye-heavy, it says so and offers the superfat that fixes it.

FREE, NO LIMITS
• Lye calculator for NaOH, KOH or dual lye, with superfat (0–20 %) and lye purity
• Water as lye concentration, water : lye ratio or % of oils, with all three shown
• Soap recipes by weight (g, oz, lb) or by percent of a total oil weight
• 23 oils, from a 100 % olive castile to a coconut-heavy laundry bar, each with its SAP range and source (Codex, USP, ARSO)
• A weigh-out checklist in pouring order, with the lye each oil takes
• Lye rounds down to the scale's precision, never up

PRO, ONE-TIME PURCHASE
• Recipe book: save unlimited soap recipes with notes, duplicate them, and share a printable batch sheet (PDF) with a weigh-out checklist
• Blend profile: fatty-acid breakdown and iodine value of your blend
• Custom oils from your supplier's SAP, fragrance by % of oils or oz per pound, and citric acid with the extra lye it takes

Built for soapers at the bench. Your working recipe saves as you type, and everything works offline.
