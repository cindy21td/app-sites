Proso Draft and Revise

How Proso works

Proso keeps your writing as plain markdown files, one per chapter or sheet, grouped into projects. Changes you, your editor or a reader make are kept on the page as marks, signed with initials in the margin, until you accept or reject them.

To see all of this on a real chapter, open Settings › Take the first pass again. It opens a fresh copy of the sample chapter, marked up by an editor and a reader, and walks through revising it.

The page

  • The line across the top is the slug line: which galley this is, the words you wrote today and your progress this week.
  • The ribbon hanging from the top edge is your week. Tap it to see the week, or to set a goal if the project has none.
  • Aa sets the paper, the typeface and the page: line spacing, page width, and spaced or indented paragraphs. Turn on Focus there to hide everything but the words.
  • Swipe in from the right edge of the page to open the next chapter.

Revising

  • Tap the pencil (Revise) to start a pass. From then on, what you delete is struck through and what you add is underlined, so nothing is lost until you decide.
  • Each changed paragraph has a bar in the margin, with the initials of whoever changed it: YOU, an editor's initials, or Qy for a line edit query.
  • Swipe a marked paragraph right to accept it, or left to reject it. You can also use the tick and cross in the strip above the page, which step through the marks one by one, or Accept all and Reject all from the ··· menu in the strip.
  • Tap the names in the strip to see one person's changes, or to accept or reject everything they did at once.
  • When the last mark in a chapter is settled, the chapter is stamped Passed for press and shows as PASSED in the book view.
  • On a Mac, the Revise menu has the same commands, with keyboard shortcuts.

Your editor's changes

  • ··· › Send for edits sends a chapter as a Word file, with your open changes and notes tracked. People you have sent to before are listed first.
  • When the file comes back, use ··· › Bring back edits and pick it. Their tracked changes and comments land on the page as marks and notes under their name, in their own colour. Where you both changed a paragraph, your wording stays and theirs follows as a note.
  • Receiving edits is free. Sending a Word file comes with the clothbound edition.

The book view

  • Go back from the page to see the book: every chapter with its state, PASSED, IN PASS or Draft, and your week laid flat.
  • Tap the book's title to switch to another project.

Drafts, cuttings and line edits

  • History and drafts keeps every version of a sheet on this device: all of the last 24 hours, then one a day for 30 days. Save a named draft at any time, and compare the chapter with it, either as the page or only the changes. Bring back a single paragraph or the whole sheet.
  • Cuttings keeps the paragraphs you cut while revising. The cuts chip on the slug line puts a cut back where it was.
  • Line edit reads a chapter on your device the way a line editor would: doubled words, dialogue tags, stock phrases, filter words, fillers, adverbs, echoes and your own words to watch. Each one is left as a query with its reason. Clear a query, or stop it asking about a phrase.

Sharing and exports

  • Share or print sends a sheet as PDF, markdown or plain text, copies it as rich text, or copies clean HTML for the web, ready to paste into a blog or a fanfiction site.
  • Markdown with marks keeps your revision marks as CriticMarkup, readable in any text editor.
  • For the whole project: standard manuscript format as Word or PDF, and EPUB for readers. These come with the clothbound edition.

Free and clothbound

Proso is free for up to three projects, with the Proof and Night papers. The clothbound edition is one payment, with no subscription. It adds every project you'll ever start, manuscript and EPUB exports, sending to your editor as Word, the page settings, seven more papers and three more typefaces, goals and the Home Screen widget, and every structure.

Your files

Your files are plain markdown, in iCloud Drive › Proso when iCloud is on, or on this device when it isn't. You can open them in the Files app or any text editor. Proso has no account.
