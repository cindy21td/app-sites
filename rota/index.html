Proso Draft and Revise

Revision Marks for Novelists

Your editor's changes, in your own manuscript. Send a chapter to your editor, your agent or a reader as a Word file with your changes tracked. When it comes back, open it in Proso: their changes and comments land on your page as revision marks, each person in their own colour, and you accept or reject them one by one. Your book stays plain markdown files that you own.

REVISION MARKS, BY NAME
Tap Revise and your own edits stay on the page as marks too: cut words struck through, new words underlined, a bar in the margin beside every changed paragraph. Every mark knows who made it. Their initials sit in the margin beside every paragraph they changed. Swipe a marked paragraph right to accept it, left to reject it, or accept all of Jo's changes at once. Settle the last mark and the chapter is stamped passed for press.

SEND, AND BRING BACK
Write on while a chapter is away: when it comes back, Proso merges paragraph by paragraph. What only they changed arrives as their marks, what only you changed stays yours, and where you both rewrote a paragraph theirs sits beside yours as a note. Nothing they wrote is lost.

THE MARKS LIVE IN THE FILE
Marks are written into the chapter itself in CriticMarkup, a plain-text convention, with each person's initials. Open the chapter in Files or on your Mac and the pass is still there.

COMPARE DRAFTS
Save a draft of the whole book under a name, "Second draft", and set any chapter against it: changed paragraphs are marked word by word, and each can take its older wording back on its own.

A LINE EDIT
Proso reads a chapter on your device, as fiction: filler, filter words (she saw, he felt), adverbs on dialogue tags, echoes, stock phrases. A tally shows where they cluster before anything touches the page; then each query sits on the page as a pencil mark with its reason.

THE BOOK
Every chapter at a glance: passed, in a pass with whose marks, or still a draft.

FINISH AND SEND
Export the book in standard manuscript format, the layout agents and magazines ask for, as Word or PDF: Times 12, double-spaced, your name and the page in the header. Make an EPUB for readers, or copy a chapter as clean text for the web.

CUTTINGS
A deleted paragraph of twelve words or more goes to the project's cuttings. Put it back in one tap.

THE PAGE
Every chapter is a galley proof, set in Iowan Old Style. Markdown symbols show only while your cursor is inside them. Choose line spacing, width and book indents, nine papers and seven typefaces.

FOR NOVELS, AND ANYTHING LONG
Projects are folders of plain .md files in your own iCloud Drive or on the device. Order chapters by hand, and keep version history for every sheet.

THE RIBBON
Set a weekly goal on a dial marked in pages and the week's ribbon fills as the words land, on the Lock Screen and Home Screen too. A writing session can run as a Live Activity.

ON THE MAC TOO
The same projects on your Mac, with menus and shortcuts.
