Proso Draft and Revise

Revision Marks for Novelists

Revise a draft and keep every change in sight. Tap Revise and your edits stay on the page as marks: the words you cut are struck through, the words you add are underlined, and a bar in the margin shows which paragraphs changed. Walk through the marks and accept or reject each one, or settle them all at once.

The marks live in the file itself, written in CriticMarkup, a plain-text convention any text editor can read. Close the app, open the chapter in Files or on your Mac, and the pass is still there.

COMPARE DRAFTS
Save a draft of the whole book under a name, "Second draft", and Proso keeps it for good. Set any chapter against an earlier draft or against any saved version of the day: the changed paragraphs are marked word by word, and each one can take its older wording back on its own.

CUTTINGS
Delete a paragraph of twelve words or more and it goes to the project's cuttings, with the chapter it came from and the paragraph it followed. Put it back in one tap, at its old place. The cuttings are a plain markdown file in the project's folder.

THE PAGE
Write in markdown without seeing it: the symbols appear only while your cursor is inside them, and a formatting bar covers what you would otherwise type. The page is set in Seravek on a bright proof white, with a word count, six looks and Focus, which clears everything but the text while you type.

FOR NOVELS, AND ANYTHING LONG
Projects are folders of plain .md files in your own iCloud Drive (or on the device), so your manuscript is yours and opens anywhere. Order chapters by hand, start from one of sixteen story, essay and journal structures, and keep version history for every sheet on your device.

THE RIBBON
Set a weekly goal on a dial marked in pages and a ribbon at the page edge fills as the words land. The ribbon also sits on the Lock Screen and the Home Screen, and a writing session can run as a Live Activity with its clock and its word count.

ON THE MAC TOO
The same projects on your Mac, with a Format menu and keyboard shortcuts.
