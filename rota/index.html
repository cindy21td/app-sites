Proso: Writing & Markdown

Plain Text Editor for Novels

Open the app and you are on the page you were writing, with the cursor where you left it.

Proso is a writing app for people with more than one thing going: a diary, a newsletter, essays, a story, a book. Every project is a folder of plain markdown files in your own iCloud Drive, so your words are yours and you can open them in Files.

THE PAGE
A calm, full-screen page set like a good paperback. Write in markdown without looking at it: the symbols show only while your cursor is inside them. A formatting bar means you never have to learn the syntax. There is a word count, three looks, a text size that follows your iPhone's, and Focus, which clears everything but your words while you type.

NEVER LOSE WRITING
Every sheet keeps its own version history on your device: all of the last 24 hours, then one a day for 30 days. Restore any version, and the text it replaces is saved first. If a sheet changes on two devices, you see both and choose. Nothing is overwritten quietly. Deleted sheets wait 30 days.

PROJECTS AND SHEETS
An Inbox plus three projects, free. Drag sheets into order for a long piece, keep the newest first for separate pieces, or start a dated sheet each day for a journal.

SHARE WHAT YOU SEE
PDF and print use the page's typeface and size. Share markdown or plain text, or copy as rich text.

PROSO PRO
One payment adds every project you will ever start; a weekly rhythm with rest days built in, shown as a ribbon on the page and on your Home Screen; four more typefaces and two more looks; and whole projects exported as one manuscript in PDF, RTF (opens in Word and Pages) or HTML.

No AI. No account. Free to write, for as long as you like.

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. Pay once and keep it. It restores on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.
