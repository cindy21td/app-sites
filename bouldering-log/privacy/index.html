Privacy policy

Sandbag Bouldering

Sandbag Bouldering works on your device and your own iCloud. It has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Data on your device

Your gyms, their tape colours, your sessions and goes are stored on your iPhone and synced through your own iCloud account (Apple CloudKit, private database) to your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID. The Live Activity for a session in progress is drawn by iOS on your device. Nothing goes to the developer. The app has no account, no analytics and no ads.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising services, and it only goes online through Apple's own services: iCloud to sync your goes and the App Store for purchases.

Purchases

The paid features are an auto-renewing subscription bought through Apple. Payments are handled by Apple and we never see your payment details. The app asks the App Store on your device whether the subscription is active; no purchase data is sent to us. Manage or cancel it in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions, and restore it on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
