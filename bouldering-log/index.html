Sandbag Bouldering

Rock climbing grades, any gym

Blue at one gym is not blue at the next. Sandbag works out what each gym's tape colours mean for you, from your own goes and sends, and hangs every gym's colours on one V-scale of your own.

Walk into any bouldering gym and the first screen answers the question you have on the mat: which colour here is your warm-up and which is your project. "Purple runs stiff here: about a V5 for you."

THE TAPE SCALE
Your grade scale runs down the left like the chart by the desk. Each gym is a lane. Each colour hangs at the grade it climbs for you, with a plain label: runs stiff, runs soft, as posted, or needs more goes. A chalk mark crosses every lane at your project line (the grade you'd expect to send in about five goes) and another at your warm-up line. Hold the scale and every tag drops back to the gym's posted chart, so you can see how far each colour moved.

ON THE MAT
No forms, no typing with chalky hands. The gym's colours come up as big strips of tape: tap Go for a go, Sent when you top it. The problem number moves on by itself after a send, Undo takes back a slip, and the scale behind you refits after every tap. A session starts on your first tap and ends when you say so or after three quiet hours. The Lock Screen shows the session with Go and Sent buttons, and Control Center has an "On the mat" button.

ANY GYM, FROM THE FIRST MINUTE
There is no gym list to wait for. Type the gym's name, tap the tape colours it uses and drag each one to its range on the gym's grade chart ("Blue V2–V4"). A sample gym is there if you want to look around first.

HOW IT WORKS OUT YOUR GRADES
Every go is a trial on one colour's set. Sandbag fits a small logistic model (the Rasch / Bradley–Terry family) to your goes, with recent goes counting more, and only labels a colour once about ten goes back it up. Your home gym is the reference scale. When the setters reset a colour, it starts a new set.

Free: two gyms, every go, your scale, the labels and both chalk lines.
Sandbag Pro adds a third gym and beyond on the same scale, keeps old sets when a gym resets ("the September blues ran stiff"), and makes a gym card image to send your crew.

No account. Your goes stay in your own iCloud and sync to your devices.

Sandbag Pro is a subscription: $3.99 a month or $24.99 a year, each starting with 7 days free. Your Apple Account is charged when the trial ends unless you cancel at least 24 hours before; the plan then renews automatically each period until you turn it off in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Restore a plan on a new phone from the punch card or the setter board.
