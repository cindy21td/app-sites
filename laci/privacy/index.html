Privacy policy

Laci Kasir Warung

Laci Kasir Warung works entirely on your device. It has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Data on your device

Your cash book (goods, sales, customers, notes and settings) is stored only on your device. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it; backups you save to Files or iCloud Drive are yours. The camera is used only when you tap the barcode button, to read a product's barcode on the device; no image is stored or sent. Bluetooth is used only to send receipts to a receipt printer next to you. Payday reminders are local notifications created on your device. WhatsApp buttons only open WhatsApp with a message filled in; you send it, the app sends nothing.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising services, and it only goes online to talk to the App Store about your purchase.

Purchases

Laci Pro (unlimited products and customers, monthly reports, CSV export) is a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. Payments are handled by Apple and we never see your payment details. Restore the purchase on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
