Lengthwise Podcasts Cut to Fit

Queue episodes for your drive

When you plug in for a 38-minute drive, Lengthwise has already cut your queue to fit: the rest of yesterday's interview, a short news episode, and the first chapter of a long one. It ends as you pull in, on a chapter, not mid-sentence.

THE TRIP STRIP
Your queue is drawn as one strip of tape at minute scale, with an arrival mark at the end of your trip. Drag the mark and the strip re-cuts as you go, snapping to chapter marks. What doesn't fit waits for the next trip. Play trip plays exactly what's on the strip, then stops.

A QUEUE YOU CONTROL
Add, remove, reorder, play next, mark played. Swipe or long-press any track. Nothing plays in the car unless you ask.

FOLLOW ANY PUBLIC SHOW
Search Apple's podcast directory, paste an Apple Podcasts link or an RSS feed URL, or bring your shows from another player as OPML. Episodes and artwork come straight from each publisher's own feed. Private feeds from a membership work by paste.

A PLAYER WITH BIG CONTROLS
Lock Screen and Control Center, headphone and car buttons, speed from 0.8x to 3x, skip intervals you choose, a sleep timer, chapters, and show notes. Controls stay full size at every text size, including the largest.

CHECKS WITHOUT A SERVER
Lengthwise checks your shows every time you open it, and in the background when iOS allows. It downloads only what's on the strip and the next trip's worth, and deletes episodes once played.

YOUR iPHONE AND iPAD IN STEP
Shows, the strip, trips and play positions sync through your own iCloud, free. On iPad and iPhone Duo's inner display, your shows, the strip and an episode sit side by side. No sign-up, no ads, no analytics.

LENGTHWISE PRO
One trip, dragged by hand and cut on chapters, is free for good. Pro adds:
- Any number of trips: Drive, Long run, Walk, the train
- Trips that learn their length from your real trips and move the mark for you
- Start a trip from Siri or Shortcuts: when your car connects, when a workout starts, when you arrive somewhere
- Show rules: skip a show's intro and outro, give it its own speed, always first, never on a trip

Lengthwise Pro costs $9.99 a year or $1.99 a month, each starting with a free week. The subscription is charged to your Apple Account when the free week ends and renews automatically unless you turn it off at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Cancel any time in Settings > your name > Subscriptions; Restore purchases is in Liner notes.
