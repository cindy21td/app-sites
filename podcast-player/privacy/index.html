Privacy policy

Lengthwise Podcasts Cut to Fit

Lengthwise Podcasts Cut to Fit works on your device and your own iCloud. It has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Data on your device

Your shows, the strip, your trips and where you are in each episode are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud sync is on in Liner notes, the app keeps a copy in your own private iCloud database so your iPhone and iPad stay in step. We cannot see either copy. Downloaded episodes stay on the device and are deleted when played. Deleting the app removes what is on the device; the iCloud copy stays with your Apple ID.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising services and has no server of ours. It goes online to fetch each show you follow from that show's own feed address (the publisher sees an ordinary request, as with any podcast player), to send a search you type to Apple's podcast directory, to iCloud for sync, and to the App Store for the subscription.

Purchases

The paid features are an auto-renewing subscription bought through Apple. Payments are handled by Apple and we never see your payment details. The app asks the App Store on your device whether the subscription is active; no purchase data is sent to us. Manage or cancel it in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions, and restore it on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
