Foilcraft: Trading Card Maker

Holo Photocard & TCG Creator

Turn any photo into a shiny holographic card, then tilt your phone and watch the foil move.

Pick a template, add your picture, choose a foil, and share it to your Instagram Story as a still or a shimmering video. Trading cards and full-bleed photocards, photo filters, custom card backs and a binder that saves every card on your device.

Your photos never leave your phone: no account, no tracking. The core app is free; one purchase unlocks Foilcraft Pro.
