Tarsi: Water Physics Puzzle

Cozy dig & rope logic puzzles

High on a misty mountain in Sulawesi, a baby tarsier has lost his family. He is small, he is brave, and he is very, very thirsty.

Tarsi is a wordless water puzzle. Swipe to dig channels through soft earth and cut ropes to set things in motion, then watch the water find its own way down, splashing, pooling and trickling, until it fills Tarsi's little bowl. It behaves just as real water would, so there is often more than one way to help.

No words, no timers, no lives. Just you, the water and a big-eyed friend who cheers when it reaches him.

FIVE CHAPTERS, ONE LONG CLIMB
Every chapter adds something new to play with, and keeps everything you have already learned.

• Rice Paddy: dig through dirt and sticky mud, and uncover boulders hidden in the hillside.
• Bamboo Groves: cut ropes to tilt pipes, pour coconuts, drop weights and crumble old gates.
• Mountain Stream: turn water to steam on hot rocks, spin waterwheels, wait for geysers, ride rafts.
• Cloud Forest: catch mist on leaves and cold stone, fill pitcher plants and swing on vines.
• The Summit: night freezes water into ice bridges, morning melts them away.

At the very top, a wordless homecoming is waiting.

GENTLE BY DESIGN
• 150 handmade levels, each with three fireflies to collect.
• A hint finger is always there if you get stuck.
• Short wordless lessons introduce each new idea.
• Soft generative gamelan music that changes with every chapter.
• Warm, chunky cartoon art, with a reduced-motion option.
• Plays fully offline. Lovely for kids and grown-ups alike.

FAIR AND SIMPLE
The first chapter, all 30 levels, is free to play. If you love it, a single one-time purchase, Tarsi Pro, unlocks chapters two to five and the ending. No ads. No subscriptions. No accounts. No tracking or data collection of any kind.
