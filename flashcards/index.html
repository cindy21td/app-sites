Morrowcard Flashcards

Spaced repetition with no cap

No daily card cap. No ads. No account. Every study mode is free, for as many cards as you have, and search finds any card from the top of the screen in one tap.

Made for students with a fixed exam date: nursing, medical and language students, and anyone sitting the bar, a CPA paper or a certification. Open the app and the first thing you read is how many of your cards will be held on exam morning: "80 of 92 held on the 12th." Under it, the run-up: every day from today to the exam drawn as a stack of card edges, one edge for each review due that day.

THE DESK
One ruled card, the prompt above the red line and the answer below it. Tap to turn, then grade with Again, Hard, Good or Easy. Each grade says when the card comes back and whether that grade holds it through exam morning, so you never wonder what a button does. Swipe right for Good, left for Again, or use the keyboard on iPad: Space turns, 1 to 4 grade, Command-Z undoes.

SCHEDULED WITH FSRS
Morrowcard schedules with FSRS-6, the open spaced repetition model, at the recall you choose for exam morning (90% by default). Cards you forget come back the same session; cards you know move out.

BRING YOUR CARDS
Import a .apkg deck saved in the older, uncompressed export format (desktop apps offer it as the option for older versions): notes, images and schedules come with it, and cards that already have a due date keep it. Or import a CSV of front, back and tags, with a preview before anything is written. Or write cards on the card itself. Export any deck as CSV, free.

BUILT FOR A BIG SCREEN TOO
On iPad and on iPhone Duo unfolded, your exams and decks sit in a sidebar, the run-up beside them, and while you study the run-up stays next to the card and redraws with every grade.

A WIDGET AND A SHORTCUT
The Home Screen widget shows today's count and days to the exam; tap it, or say "Review now in Morrowcard", to land on today's stack.

FREE
Unlimited decks and cards, every study mode (flip, type the answer, both directions), FSRS scheduling, one exam's run-up, .apkg and CSV import, CSV export, the widget.

PREMIUM
• Several exams at once, each with its own run-up and date
• The cards that won't hold on exam morning, and the daily load that fixes it
• A daily backup of your collection to your own iCloud

What Morrowcard doesn't do: sync with a desktop flashcard collection, generate cards with AI, or keep streaks.

Morrowcard Premium costs $2.99 a month or $19.99 a year, each starting with a free week. Payment is taken from your Apple Account when you confirm the subscription. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the current period ends; cancel or manage it in your iPhone's Settings under your Apple Account > Subscriptions. Already subscribed on another device? Use Restore on the exam slip.
