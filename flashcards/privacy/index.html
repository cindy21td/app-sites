Privacy policy

Morrowcard Flashcards

Morrowcard Flashcards is built to work on your device. It has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Data you enter

Your decks, cards, images and study history are stored in one file on your device; nothing is sent to us. With Premium, the app keeps a daily copy of that file in your own iCloud account (a private app folder) so you can restore it on a new phone. Decks you import stay on the device; the app has no account and no sync to other flashcard apps.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising services. It goes online only through Apple's own services: the App Store, to check whether you have an active subscription to the premium features, and iCloud for the backup. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple sends us anonymous crash reports; you can turn that off under Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Subscriptions

Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: flashcards@curiouscrane.dev.
