Day Planner by Second Column

Time blocking, old plans kept

It's 10:40 and the morning plan is gone: the meeting ran over, the deep block you protected is half used. Tap Re-block, say why, and draw the rest of the day in the next column. The broken plan stays beside it, struck from 10:45, still readable.

THE DAY PAGE
An hour rail down the edge, your plan in blocks you draw by dragging down the page. Deep work is solid ink, shallow work outlined, rest left blank. Calendar events sit on the page as fixed blocks (read only; the app never changes your calendar).

RE-BLOCK FROM NOW
One tap strikes everything after the next grid line and opens a new column with the morning carried over. Pick the cause: meeting ran over, interruption, underestimated, energy or other. Each break gets a mark on the rail. Rather nudge a block instead? Drag it up or down in the same column, or use Earlier and Later; nothing else moves.

ONE NUMBER AT NIGHT
"Kept 2 h 55 of 4 h deep." The page shows how much of the deep work you planned in the morning survived the day, and every version of the plan side by side. Hold an earlier column to see that plan alone.

THE BREAK LEDGER (PRO)
An hour by weekday map of when your plans break and why. "Tuesdays after 2:00 broke four times this month, all meetings." Cause counts and the share of deep time kept, week by week.

FREE, FOR GOOD
The whole day page, unlimited re-blocks, repeating blocks on weekdays, one saved day, calendar events. No account and no server: pages live on your iPhone.

Second Column has no timer and no to-do inbox. It plans the day and keeps what happened to the plan.
